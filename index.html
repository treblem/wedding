Our Love Story

Where Our Forever Began

✦

Our story began in Camambugan National High School, where two hearts met in the simplest of ways. At first, we were just two people sharing the same space, unaware that God was slowly writing our story.

Through ordinary days, shared smiles, conversations, and countless little moments, we grew closer. What started as a simple friendship slowly became a love that neither of us could ignore.

As our relationship grew, we learned that true love is not only about happiness, but also about choosing each other through every challenge and every season of life.

"Two are better than one… If either of them falls down, one can help the other up."

— Ecclesiastes 4:9–10

One day, we hope to stand before God, our families, and the people we love, promising to walk together for the rest of our lives.

From the place where we first met, to the day we say "I do," and through all the years that follow—our greatest story will always be the one we write together.

Melbhert & Maribeth
